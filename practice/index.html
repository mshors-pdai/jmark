Tier 1 · Daily

Three windows where sorting happens

Hypnagogic states bracket the day — priors are loosest just after waking and just before sleep. The midday endorphin window is the third high-leverage moment. Stack the deliberate work into these and skip nothing else.

☉
Morning · 25–30 min

Concentration → write

  1. Concentration (10–15 min) — focused attention on breath, mantra, or body. Builds the open state.
  2. Open monitoring (3–5 min) — sit with what arises; notice which priors are running.
  3. Visualization / intention (5–10 min) — desired self-state in present tense, with emotion if possible.
  4. Integration writing (5 min) — journal the state in past tense. "Today I was…"
⚡
Midday · exercise + 15 min after

Endorphin window

  1. Hold the morning intention during exercise.
  2. Within 15 min after, brief visualization or one paragraph of journaling.
  3. Long walks & runs work especially well — sustained DMN suppression.
  4. Post-exercise window is high-write because endorphins + BDNF + reduced cortisol.
☾
Evening · 10–15 min

Body scan → drift

  1. Body scan or yoga nidra (5–10 min) — release physical priors stored in tension.
  2. Re-read morning affirmations.
  3. Drift off holding the desired self-state — sleep consolidates whatever's held at the threshold.
Tier 2 · Weekly

The calibration tier

Daily alone drifts toward delusion without reality-testing. Weekly cadence introduces the challenge that calibrates the sort, plus the longer practice that reaches what daily can't.

One longer session

  • 60–90 min sustained practice
  • Deeper sit, breathwork (Wim Hof or holotropic), or restorative yoga
  • Long walk-meditation also works
  • Reaches priors that daily can't touch

One real-world reality-test

  • Deliberately enter a situation that tests the new sort
  • Public speaking, difficult conversation, athletic competition, creative submission
  • Without this, the sort drifts toward fantasy
  • Pick contests you can survive AND that calibrate within range

Bonded intimate connection

  • With a partner you're sorting toward
  • Post-coital window is high-write for relationship priors
  • Intention before · integration after (presence, talking, eye contact)
  • Partner-specific; not a substitute for personal practice

Sleep optimization

  • 7–9 hours, regular schedule
  • Dark room, no screens 60 min before bed
  • REM is the consolidation organ
  • Non-negotiable — defeats every other tier if neglected
Tier 3 · Monthly · Quarterly · Annual

The deeper windows

Less frequent, higher amplitude. These reach priors that ordinary open states can't dislodge. Required less often than people assume; useless without the daily infrastructure to consolidate them.

Monthly · one extended session

  • 3+ hours sustained practice
  • Long hike + meditation, half-day at home phone-off, half-day workshop
  • Lets the open state extend long enough to reach what daily can't

Quarterly · environment shift

  • 2–3 day retreat alone or in a structured setting
  • Removes competing observers; lets the sort settle
  • Trip-as-practice — see the Trips section for examples

Annual (optional) · deeper intervention

  • Psychedelic-assisted work for stuck priors
  • Therapeutic container · set, setting, and integration matter as much as substance
  • NOT recreational — the trip is the opening; integration is where the work happens
  • ~1–2× per year max

Trip as protocol

  • A weekend itinerary and a day's practice share the same shape
  • Open the window · set the intention · execute · integrate
  • See /trip/ — each itinerary is a multi-day practice container
Tier 4 · Substrate

What the practice runs on

The nervous-system substrate is affected by ordinary biology. Practice without a clean substrate is practice against a leaky bucket.

Diet

Anti-inflammatory baseline. Gut microbiome → vagus → brain. Inflammation reduces neuroplasticity.

Cold exposure

Showers or plunges. Controlled hormesis — mild stress builds resilience capacity.

Morning sunlight

10–20 min in the first hour after waking. Regulates circadian, dopamine, vitamin D.

Social environment

The nervous systems near you calibrate yours via co-regulation. Most underestimated lever.

Information diet

Every input is a sort being applied. Algorithmic feeds are captive content; curate ruthlessly.

Movement

Daily exercise. Endorphins, BDNF, DMN suppression. Substrate AND practice window.

Anti-protocol

What to avoid

Captive-content states, write-maladaptive substances, environments that overwrite private gains. Avoiding these is half the practice.

  • Heavy or daily alcohol — writes habit priors; impairs hippocampal consolidation.
  • Daily THC — read-without-write; state-dependent encoding; blunts motivation chronically.
  • Recreational opioids, cocaine, amphetamines — captive content; the substance writes itself as the prior.
  • Doom-scrolling, dopamine apps — write-maladaptive priors; algorithmic capture.
  • Toxic relationships — write the prior "I am someone these people treat this way."
  • Skipping sleep — defeats everything else; REM is the consolidation organ.
  • Reading without testing — drift toward intellectual ungroundedness; sort needs challenge.
Modalities

Ranked by leverage

Where to spend the next hour, ranked. The 70-20-10 rule: daily work is 70% of the result; weekly challenge 20%; dramatic intervention 10%. People reverse the proportions and wonder why they don't progress.

01

Daily morning meditation + writing

Highest leverage. Skip nothing else and you're 70% there.

02

Exercise + endorphin window

Second-highest. Substrate-improving, write-positive, free.

03

Sleep

Substrate consolidation. Defeats everything else if neglected.

04

Real-world challenge

Calibration. Without this, daily work drifts into delusion.

05

Social environment

Passive but powerful. Choose the rooms whose nervous systems calibrate yours where you're going.

06

Bonded sex

Partner-work for relationship priors. Oxytocin write window after.

07

Cold exposure / breathwork

Accessory tools with real effects. Cheap weekly additions.

08

Psychedelics / plant medicines

Occasional deep intervention. Overrated in popular discourse — daily infrastructure is what makes their gains stick.

09

Floats / ice baths / fasting

Situational, not foundational.

10

Recreational substances

Net negative for sorting. Use sparingly if at all; never as part of the protocol.

The Compression

Daily concentration + observation + intention + writing in open states. Weekly challenge + bonded connection + longer session. Quarterly retreat. Annual deeper intervention if warranted. Substrate: sleep, sun, movement, food, social. Avoid captive content.

The 70-20-10 rule: daily work is 70% of the result; weekly challenge is 20%; dramatic intervention is 10%. People reverse the proportions and wonder why they don't progress.

Sort yourself. Test the sort. Repeat.
