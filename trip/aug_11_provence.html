Only This Week

Don't-Miss Events on Your Exact Dates

Four experiences that only happen during the week you're there. Plan the itinerary around these.

☄️

Perseid Meteor Shower Peak

Night of Wed Aug 12 → Thu Aug 13

The best Perseid year of the decade. New moon on Aug 12 = moonless sky, 90+ meteors/hour at peak (1–4 AM local). Drive to Cap Canaille after midnight — France's highest sea cliff, 394m, zero light pollution toward the sea. Bring blankets, thermoses, a bottle of Bandol rosé. Plan to be back at hotel by 4 AM.

Viewing guide →
🎆

Assumption Day in Cassis

Sat Aug 15 — French national holiday

Cassis throws its biggest party of the year. 7 PM apéro-concert with band "Years" at Place Baragnon. 10 PM the fireworks at Port de Cassis — they're famous for the pyrotechnic quality. 10:30 PM the August 15 Ball at Place Baragnon. Stay in town this evening — no Aix or Marseille trip on Saturday.

Event details →
🎹

Festival de La Roque d'Anthéron

Runs through your trip until Aug 16

The world's largest piano festival — 46th edition, ~100 concerts in the Parc de Florans under giant sequoias. 1h drive from Cassis. Tickets released April 2026; book a concert on Day 5 (Aug 16) as a refined alternative to a beach day. Final-week recitals are typically the highest-caliber.

Program →
🎨

Cassis Night Market & Quai des Artistes

Every evening in your week, 7 PM–midnight

Place Baragnon and Place Clémenceau fill with local painters, portraitists, caricaturists, and engravers from 7 PM to midnight, June 25 → Aug 31. Best after dinner — wander through, watch artists work, occasionally something is worth taking home. Free; built into your evening walks.

Market info →

💜 Bonus — Sault lavender (the ambitious add-on)

Most Provence lavender is harvested by mid-July. But the Sault plateau (800m elevation, above the Mont Ventoux foothills) holds bloom into mid-August by tradition — locals don't cut until the Aug 15 Lavender Harvest Festival. The catch: it's a 2-hour drive from Cassis (above the 1.5h budget). Doable as a long day trip if you really want to see the fields — leave at dawn, return for the Cassis fireworks. Sault tourism →

The Glass

Two AOCs, 30 Minutes Apart

The whites of Cassis and the reds of Bandol — both small, both serious, and both rarely poured outside the region. Most visits are by appointment; details below.

Cassis AOC · the whites

France's First AOC (1936)

Mineral, herb-scented whites from Clairette and Marsanne, ripened on terraces between the limestone cliffs and the sea. Only 12 estates produce Cassis AOC. The microclimate — sheltered by Cap Canaille's wind shadow — is what makes the wines unmistakable. Most estates are 5–15 minutes from the port; some you can walk to.

Clos Sainte Magdeleine ↗
On the cliff over the Med — arguably Provence's most beautiful winery setting. Tue–Sat 10–12 & 3–7. €12 visit, +€3 tasting. +33 4 42 01 70 28.
Domaine du Bagnol ↗
Tiny producer, walking distance from town. The benchmark Cassis white. +33 4 42 01 32 16.
Domaine du Paternel ↗
Third-generation Santini family. Vineyards in Cassis, Bandol AND Saint-Cyr — taste all three from one cellar.
Château de Fontblanche ↗
Long-established Cassis house, less polished than the others — and more honest for it.
Bandol AOC · the reds

Mourvèdre Country — 30 min East

Bandol is Provence taken seriously. At least 50% Mourvèdre in every red and rosé — a grape that needs heat to ripen and gives back wines that age for decades. The vineyards climb amphitheaters above the sea. This is the Provence wine list at three-star restaurants in Paris, made by families you've never heard of.

Domaine Tempier ★ ↗
The legend. Mon–Fri 9–12 & 1:30–5:30. Book online or call +33 4 94 98 70 21. Tastings of red, rosé, and white. Le Plan du Castellet.
Château de Pibarnon ↗
Highest vineyards in Bandol (300m). Panoramic terrace tastings, elegant age-worthy reds. By appointment, Mon–Sat. +33 4 94 90 12 73.
Domaine de Terrebrune ↗
Biodynamic since the 1970s. Reds with extraordinary aging potential. Appointment only.
Château Sainte-Anne ↗
Family estate near La Cadière. Classic Bandol style, generous welcome, lower profile than Tempier.

⚠ August crowd note

Cassis is at peak crowd in your week. Mitigation built into this itinerary: (1) early-morning boat departures (8:30 AM), (2) sunrise hikes, (3) Sunday day-trip to Aix when day-trippers are sleeping, (4) winery appointments mid-week. You'll see the busy port — you won't get stuck in it.
