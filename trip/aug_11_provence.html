⚠️ Fire-closure note — the Calanques' August asterisk

The Calanques National Park sets daily fire-access status the prior evening 17:00–18:00 between Jun 1 and Sep 30. Green = open all-day. Yellow/Orange = access 06:00–11:00 only. Red = land access totally forbidden (Route des Crêtes closed; boats sail but no landing). Black = full closure. August averages multiple red days, especially after heatwaves. Check the MesCalanques app or calanques-parcnational.fr the night before any hike day. The Sat Aug 15 dawn hike (Port-Miou → En Vau) is the most at-risk day; the swap to a full Aix day with Atelier Cézanne 11:00 guided tour + Cours Mirabeau lunch is fluid. The L'Eden Boat RIB day is more weather-resilient — it sails in marginal conditions where land hikes are closed.

Adversarial honesty — where Provence loses

1 · Cassis is a postcard village, not a nightlife destination. Last dinner seatings 21:00–21:30. One real nightclub. Every "real" late night requires a 35-min Marseille drive + Uber back. Mallorca's Palma / San Sebastián Old Town / Sorrento all eat Cassis on this axis. If the brief is "Joann thinks this shit boring make it active with nightlife," Basque is honestly a better fit by ~3 points — mostly via Nightlife (+1.5) + Joann-seafood (+1.5) + Food (+0.5 if max).

2 · August in the Calanques is structurally fragile. Fire-closure red days happen multiple times/week in heatwave years. Boat day is red-day-resistant; hike day is not. You may genuinely lose the En Vau hike. Heat compresses outdoor windows to 06–11 + 17–21 most days. Strong on paper, brittle in execution.

3 · Cassis IS bouillabaisse capital. Half the Michelin you've heard of (Villa Madie / Petit Nice / L'Épuisette / AM) is seafood-forward. Even Une Table au Sud is Mediterranean-leaning. Unless Joann pivots toward "I'll order the lamb" on every menu, the dining itinerary is constantly negotiated.

4 · No nonstop LAX → MRS. Minimum 1 connection (usually CDG). Adds 4–5 hours friction each end vs. nonstops to Barcelona / Madrid / Lisbon.

5 · Piano festival is gorgeous but cerebral. 1h15 drive each way for 90 minutes of Brahms is not what someone bored by sleepy Cassis wants. The Aug 16 jazz night (Thomas Enhco + Vassilena Serafimova) is the only program that fits the "active + nightlife" profile — high-energy crossover, marimba + piano duo, perfect closer.

vs. Mallorca: Mallorca delivers what Cassis can't — walk-out-of-hotel-into-nightlife-at-23:00 (Palma + Nikki Beach + Puro + Mhares beach clubs). Belmond La Residencia is the hotel ceiling. ★★★ Michelin coverage similar (Voro ★★ at Park Hyatt Mallorca + several ★). Pick Mallorca for resort-nightlife couples.

vs. Basque: San Sebastián has the densest ★ count per capita on Earth. Joann is meat-forward (chuleta + jamón + pintxos = no negotiation). Pintxo crawl IS nightlife (no 35-min Marseille drive). Rioja day is shorter + meatier than Bandol. Pick Basque if food + Joann-comfort are the top axes.

But: Cassis is BOOKED. Villa Madie ★★★ is BOOKED. L'Eden Boat is BOOKED. Deposits are sunk. The play is to maximize Provence on its actual strengths: add Marseille for nightlife depth, add Roque d'Anthéron for cultural energy, add Bandol for wine, let Cassis carry the romance + wake-up-at-the-port piece. That's what the over-packed itinerary above does.
