XLSIOR Festival Aug 20–25, 2026 — overlaps 5 of 5 nights

The world's largest gay-circuit festival lands on Mykonos Aug 20–25, programmed across Cavo Paradiso, Jackie O' Beach, SantAnna, Super Paradise, and pop-up venues. ~30,000 attendees from 70+ countries descend on Chora — Mykonos's gay-capital identity goes into overdrive. Mykonos Summer Festival

Runs throughout August 2026 — open-air at Kato Mili windmills + Archaeological Museum courtyard

The municipality's open-air cultural festival — classical and Greek folk concerts, traditional dance, theatre, contemporary art installations — programmed at heritage venues across Chora: the Kato Mili windmill row (the 16th-century landmark, 7 of the original 16 windmills still on the hill above Little Venice), the Archaeological Museum courtyard, and Manto Mavrogenous Square. Programs land late June; expect 2–3 evening events per week through August at €0–€20. Provides a genuinely quiet civic-culture counterweight to the XLSIOR week intensity — most events are at the windmill row or the museum and run 20:30–22:30.

Panagia Tourliani · Ano Mera year-round

Year-round — 1542 monastery · post-Dekapentavgoustos calm · tavernas + bouzouki nights

The 1542 monastery in Ano Mera village center — the marquee Dekapentavgoustos Panigyri (Aug 14–15) is past by your arrival, but the post-feast week (Aug 17–21) is the village's calmest stretch of summer. The square is still set with long tables most evenings, traditional Cycladic music (bouzouki + violin + laouto) plays in 2–3 tavernas, and the monastery courtyard is open 09:00–13:00 + 17:00–19:00 for visitors. The non-XLSIOR escape valve for anyone who wants a Cycladic village dinner with grilled lamb + house wine instead of a circuit party.

Assyrtiko, Athiri, Malagouzia

Mykonos itself is not a wine destination — but the broader Cyclades is one of Greece's most distinctive wine zones. Mykonos Vioma in Ano Mera is the only working organic vineyard on the island (biodynamic since 1994, planted to Asyrtiko / Athiri / Malagouzia / Mandilaria). With Dekapentavgoustos past, the Aug 17–23 window is the year's best Vioma visit window — vintage is in, the harvest team is back from the fields, the tour-plus-farm-lunch slots are bookable 3 days ahead instead of 14. Santorini is the bigger trophy but the no-overnight Aug schedule means a same-day round-trip from Mykonos isn't viable (last ferry back is ~18:00). The wine ceiling is real but the catalog is small versus Mallorca or the Côte d'Azur.
