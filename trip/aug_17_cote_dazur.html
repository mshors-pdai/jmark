Cannes Festival d'Art Pyrotechnique FINALE · Flash Barrandov (Czech Republic)

Mon Aug 24 · 22:00 · the closing-night fireworks + Golden Vestale awards ceremony · ⚠️ extend trip 1 night to catch

The single best reason to extend. The Cannes Festival d'Art Pyrotechnique is the most prestigious international fireworks competition in the world — four nights through July + August, each a different national team. Aug 24 is the FINALE — Flash Barrandov from the Czech Republic — followed by the Golden Vestale awards ceremony for the four-night competition. ~20-minute show, free public viewing, launched from barges directly in front of the Hôtel Carlton on La Croisette. Free standing on the Croisette sand or paid front-row dinner at one of the beach clubs (Z Plage Martinez, Plage 39 Carlton, La Plage du Festival). Crowds top 200,000; Croisette closes to vehicles from 19:00. Take the late train back to Nice (last TER ~01:00) or move one night to Hôtel Martinez Cannes for the Croisette-front sleep. Extending to Tue Aug 25 departure adds ~$1,500 but boosts the trip score from 32 to 34.

Casino de Monte-Carlo · Belle Époque · daily 14:00–04:00

Wed/Thu/Fri/Sat Aug 19–22 = Jimmy'z eligible nights post-Casino

The 1863 Belle Époque casino, Place du Casino — Salons Européens, Privés, Touzet, Salle Médecin. €18–€20 entry to enter the gaming rooms. Jacket required after 20:00 for men; no shorts/sneakers/sportswear after 14:00; T-shirts mornings only. Walk the rooms even if you don't play — this is one of the world's last operational Belle Époque casinos. The play: train Nice → Monaco (25 min, €5), pre-Casino aperitif at Bar Américain (Hôtel de Paris, historic Belle Époque, live jazz) or Crystal Bar Hermitage, 30 min at the European roulette table, walk 7 min to Jimmy'z for the late slot. Last train back to Nice ~01:00; otherwise late taxi €80 or sleep at Hôtel Columbus Monaco.

Jimmy'z Monte-Carlo · Wed–Sat in Jul/Aug · 23:00–05:00

Eligible: Wed Aug 19 / Thu Aug 20 / Fri Aug 21 / Sat Aug 22 · smart-casual strict

The Riviera disco institution since 1971, Avenue Princesse Grace, the Jul/Aug summer schedule is Wed–Sat 23:00–05:00 (not Wed–Sun as some older guides list). Four of your six nights are eligible. Cover €30/pp at the door, tables via SBM concierge (3–4 weeks lead). The crowd is Monaco–Russian–French old money + Formula 1 weekenders + the post-Louis-XV ★★★ residue. Smart-casual heavily policed — no shorts, no sneakers, no t-shirts. Sat Aug 22 is the peak. Pair with the Casino + Louis XV or La Chèvre d'Or earlier. Last train back to Nice doesn't run after 01:00 — plan a taxi (~€80) or stay at Hôtel Columbus Monaco for the night.

Nice hills + Lérins · the local-only pours

Bellet AOC + Saint-Honorat Monks

Bellet is the smallest French urban AOC — ~50 hectares in the hills above Nice, barely 13 producers, almost all consumed locally. The only AOC in France entirely within a city's limits. The signature: Folle Noire (red) + Rolle (white, the same grape as Vermentino in Italy). The producer to visit is Château de Crémat — 19th-century pink-stucco castle, sit-down tasting in the cellar room, Mon–Sat by appointment. 20 min drive from the Negresco. Lérins Abbey on Saint-Honorat (the second of the Lérins ferry pair) is a working Cistercian vineyard — 8 ha, 35,000 bottles/yr, 5 cuvées + the herbal liqueur Lérina. The first-Friday-of-the-month public monks-and-wine tour falls Aug 7 (before our window) + Sep 4 (after) — but the abbey shop is open daily and self-visit walking the vineyard is available throughout your visit.

Adversarial honesty — where this downscaled Côte d'Azur stands

Even downscaled, Côte d'Azur is the most expensive option in the set. The Standard tier here at ~$13,000 still runs ~1.4× Cascais Standard (~$9,500) and ~1.3× Annecy Standard (~$10,000). The Riviera Michelin density + structural luxury of the Promenade + Casino + Cap Ferrat is what you're paying for. The Aug 11–17 splurge version ran $20K–$50K — this version proves the trip is doable in band, just barely.

vs. Cascais Aug 17–23 (~$11K): Cascais scores 37.5 — leads at lower cost with Festas do Mar overlap, Praia da Conceição beach, José Avillez's tasting menus. Cascais wins on score-per-dollar. Côte d'Azur wins on Casino + Caves du Roy + Riviera mythology.

vs. Annecy Aug 17–23 (~$10K): Annecy scores 35–36 with the Imperial Festival fireworks Aug 22 + cooler Alps temps + Lac d'Annecy swimming. Annecy wins on weather + price. Côte d'Azur wins on Michelin density + nightlife.

What you lose vs. the Aug 11–17 splurge version: (1) Eden-Roc + Cheval Blanc + Hôtel de Paris are out — Negresco still gorgeous but a step down on hotel romance. (2) You skip Le Louis XV ★★★ (or you eat it AND eat La Chèvre d'Or ★★ at extra cost). (3) The Aug 15 Cannes fireworks anchor is past — extending to Aug 24 partially substitutes with the FINALE (Flash Barrandov + Golden Vestale awards) but it's a 1-night extension cost (~$1,500). (6) Jimmy'z Jul/Aug is Wed–Sat NOT Wed–Sun — older guides wrong. (7) Last train Nice → Monaco is ~01:00; late taxi €80 if leaving Jimmy'z at 04:00.
