Don't-Miss Events on Your Exact Dates

Four experiences that only happen during the week you're there. The Perseids over Col de la Forclaz is the headline.

☄️

Perseids from Col de la Forclaz

Night of Wed Aug 12 → Thu Aug 13

The best Perseid year of the decade. New moon Aug 12 = moonless sky, 90+ meteors/hour at peak (1–4 AM). Drive 30 min up to Col de la Forclaz — the paragliding launch site at 4,500 ft above Lake Annecy, looking south down the full length of the lake to the Tournette and Bauges mountains. Dark sky, restaurant terrace open late. Bring a fleece and a thermos of génépi.

Viewing guide →
⛪

Assomption · Fêtes Mariales

Sat Aug 15 — French national holiday

Major French Catholic holiday. Religious processions through the old town to the Basilique de la Visitation on the hill. Most shops closed. Restaurants packed (book Yoann Conte for this night well ahead). The town is alive — Place Saint-François fills with markets and street musicians. Evening boat lights on the lake; smaller hotels run special Assumption Day menus.

Lake Annecy tourism →
🎵

Annecy Classic Festival

Mid-August in the Imperial Palace gardens

Chamber music + opera + recitals through mid-August in lakeside venues — the gardens of Impérial Palace, the Cathédrale Saint-Pierre, château courtyards. Sunset concerts on the lake at 8 PM. Tickets €25–€80. Pairs naturally with a Day 4 or Day 5 dinner before/after. The most refined evening Annecy offers.

🧀

Alpine cheese harvest season

Aug = peak alpage season

August is when the alpage (high-pasture) cheeses are at their peak — cows have been grazing wildflowers since June. The summer-milk Reblochon, Beaufort d'été, Tomme des Bauges are at their best right now. Visit an alpage producer (Coopérative laitière du Val d'Aulps, Fromagerie Pingon) — they run morning tastings July–August only. Pair with an Alpine drive.

The Glass & Plate

Cheese, Bread, Wine — Not Seafood

The Savoyard food culture is built on Alpine cheese (Reblochon, Beaufort, Tomme), cured meats (saucisson, jambon, diots), crusty bread, and crisp Vin de Savoie whites. Lake fish (omble chevalier, féra) plays a supporting role — fresher and lighter than sea fish.

The cheese trail · 4 AOP cheeses

Reblochon · Beaufort · Tomme · Abondance

Four Savoie AOP cheeses, all made within 90 minutes of the lake. August is peak alpage season — the cows have been grazing wildflowers since June, the summer cheese is at its best. Producers run morning tastings July–August only.

Fromagerie Pingon (Manigod) ↗
Alpage Reblochon producer 45 min east of Annecy. Cave tour + tasting 3-month vs 6-month, $15 pp. Tue–Sat 10 AM–noon.
Coopérative Beaufortain ↗
Beaufortain valley, 45 min SE. Beaufort d'été (summer Beaufort), 12/18/24-month bottlings. Daily tour 10:30 AM. €8.
Tomme producers Bauges ↗
Tomme de Savoie + Tome des Bauges — the everyday cheese. Many small producers; Marchello at Bauges (drop-in OK Tue–Sat 9–1).
Mercato des Halles, Annecy
Tue/Fri/Sun mornings, central Annecy. All four AOP cheeses + Savoyard charcuterie under one roof. Best one-stop tasting if not doing the alpage drive.
Vin de Savoie · the Alpine whites

Apremont · Chignin-Bergeron · Roussette

The Alpine wines are crisp, mineral, lightly fizzy — built to drink with fondue, raclette, and lake fish. Apremont (from Jacquère grape) is the classic; Chignin-Bergeron (Roussanne) is the structured one; Roussette de Savoie (Altesse) is the most age-worthy. All within 1h drive of Annecy in the Combe de Savoie.

Domaine Belluard ↗
Cult Ayze producer — late Dominique Belluard's natural-wine estate, sparkling Gringet a benchmark. By appointment only.
Domaine des 13 Lunes (Apremont)
Biodynamic Jacquère + Mondeuse, 45 min south. Sat tastings + lunch on the terrace. By appointment.
Domaine Gilles Berlioz (Chignin-Bergeron)
Benchmark Chignin-Bergeron producer in Chignin (40 min). Tastings by appointment Tue–Sat.
Génépi des Pères Chartreux (Voiron, 1h 30m)
The Alpine herb liqueur. Distillery tour at Grande Chartreuse — the only place that knows the recipe of green and yellow Chartreuse. Optional Day 5 detour.
