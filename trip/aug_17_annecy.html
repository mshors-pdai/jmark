The Itinerary

One base for the whole trip — 5 nights at L'Impérial Palace Annecy (Mon Aug 17 → Sat Aug 22) + 1 final night (Sat Aug 22 → Sun Aug 23). Only This Week

Imperial Annecy Festival · The Six-Night Anchor

The 11th edition of the Imperial Annecy Festival runs Aug 18–28, 2026 at the Imperial Palace — exactly the venue we'd sleep in. Six festival nights overlap the trip (Tue Aug 18 → Sun Aug 23). Perseids residual tail

Active through Aug 24 · peak past Aug 12–13

The Perseids new-moon peak fell Aug 12–13 — 5 days before you land. Residual meteors continue through Aug 24 but at maybe 10–20/hr not the 90+/hr peak. Col de la Forclaz is still a great dark-sky perch (1,147 m, paragliding overlook, ★★ Auberge de Montmin at the col) — worth a Day 3 dinner + late terrace sit on the off-chance, but not the structural anchor it was the week prior. The decade-best moonless show belongs to the Aug 11–17 itinerary; this trip trades it for the Imperial Festival.

⚠️ What you miss — and a fact-check on the "Annecy Classic Festival"

Fête du Lac d'Annecy 2026 "L'Âme du Monde" — Europe's largest pyrotechnic show, ~200,000 spectators — fell Sat Aug 1, 16 days before you land. Fête des Guides de Chamonix 2026 Aug 12–16 — ceremony day fell Sat Aug 15, the day before departure. Le Brise Glace (Annecy's main contemporary music venue, 500-cap) reopens Mon Aug 24 — miss by 1 day. Édouard Baer Aug 28 at the Festival — the most universally-stage-presence headliner — falls 5 days after departure (Caverivière Aug 23 is French stand-up = language barrier). And the "Annecy Classic Festival Aug 23–27" sometimes cited in older guidebooks DOES NOT EXIST in 2026 — the original Annecy Classic Festival was discontinued in 2016 after Russian patron Andreï Cheglakov pulled funding; the successor "Variations Classiques" runs Jan–Mar at Bonlieu in winter only. Talloires Abbey hosts its own classical concert series Aug 29–31, also after departure. Don't assume the Classic Festival is happening — it isn't.

Arrival · Easy Lake Day · MS Libellule dinner cruise · Casino nightcap

  • 11:35 AM Land ZRH · 1h25 layover · 12:55 PM LX2810 boards
  • 1:55 PM Land GVA · rental car (Hertz/Sixt, ~$380 for 6 days, auto) — 40-min drive A41 south to Annecy
  • 3:30 PM Check in L'Impérial Palace on the lakefront — Belle Époque 1913, 90 rooms + 14 suites · request lake-view room facing Albigny Bay · 15% discount if booked >21 days out direct · the Festival venue itself starts tomorrow night
  • 4:30 PM Walk Plage d'Albigny (5 min) — lake swim 22–25 °C "cleanest in Europe" · kayak walk-up €11/h from Mini Golf de l'Impérial at Petit Port

  • 6:30 PM Apéro at La Perla Plage Lounge lakeside (or Ô Cèdre rooftop at Hôtel Le Pélican) — gins/cocktails, lake view
  • 7:30 PM Walk back to Imperial Palace · freshen up · stroll to Pâquerie d'Annecy ferry terminal
  • 7:45 PM Board MS Libellule "Dragonfly" dinner cruise · Compagnie des Bateaux du Lac d'Annecy · electric boat, 2-h cruise 19:30 boarding · Dragonfly menu €66.60/pp (Escapade €59 · Couronne de Savoie €91.50) · Annecy → Talloires → return · the no-cook arrival night · book 1–2 weeks +33 4 50 51 08 40

Alternative · Low energy: Skip the dinner cruise. Walk to Le Freti (12 Rue Sainte-Claire, Savoyard institution since 1974) for 19:00 — 15+ fondue variants raw-milk farmhouse cheese · unlimited raclette by wheel · their signature "Brisgotte" (fondue in a bread bowl with bacon-wrapped potatoes). €55/pp. The "we just flew 11 hours, give us cheese and bed" play.
Alternative · Doubling down: Push earlier and grab a daytime Compagnie des Bateaux Crouy-class lake tour (90-min, walk-up at the harbor) before dinner — circles the southern half of the lake in daylight, gives you orientation, leaves you ready for a proper land-based dinner at Chez Mamie Lise or L'Étage (modern Savoyard, Vieille Ville) instead of the cruise.
Alternative · Rainy day: Cruise cancels in heavy storm but boards in light rain. If full cancel: dinner at Chez Ingalls (city center · "continuous service until midnight" · the Annecy answer to a 22:00 sit-down) + Casino tables till close. Or pivot to Le M Annecy modern Savoyard for a comfortable indoor finish.
