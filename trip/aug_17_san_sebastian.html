What you trade by booking this week

The Festival Deflation

Aste Nagusia 2026 runs Aug 8–15. You land 48 hours after the closing-night fireworks. The city is finishing its post-festival cleanup the morning you arrive. Here's exactly what's gone — and what's still here, which is genuinely most of the trip.

Already happened

Five Anchors That Made the Aug 11 Window the Leader

All five lifted the Aug 11–17 score on the hard ×2 nightlife and activity axes. This week reverts SS to the off-peak baseline.

Gone

Aste Nagusia · 60th International Fireworks Competition

Eight straight nightly 22:45 fireworks shows over La Concha · the 60-year tradition the city is named for in summer · closing finale Sat Aug 15 was 48 hours before you land. No fireworks any night of your trip.

Gone

Txosnas (festival bar tents) on the Boulevard

Six official festival-tent zones open till 04:00 Aug 8–15. Kalimotxo (red wine + cola), live DJ stages, Plaza Trinidad free concerts. All packed up by Sun Aug 16. SS reverts to its normal nightlife reality: pintxos till ~midnight, then a handful of late bars + Bataplán.

Gone

Asunción de la Virgen · Aug 15 holiday + procession

The Spanish national-holiday procession from Iglesia de Santa María del Coro that historically bookends Aste Nagusia. By Aug 17 the city is firmly back to a normal Monday.

Gone

Perseids peak + 2026 new moon

Aug 12 was the year's best meteor shower window — moonless, 90+ meteors/hour. By Aug 17 the moon is a first-quarter crescent, sky brightness ruins meteor visibility, and the shower's main peak has dropped to background-rate trails.

Gone

Sokolov Quincena Musical recital

The marquee Aug 13 piano date with Grigory Sokolov is gone. Quincena Musical continues through Aug 30 with other artists, but the headline summer recital that anchored the cultural night is past.

Gone

The "tied leader" identity

The Aug 11–17 page had SS competing for the overall best European week. This page does not. Re-scored on the same 8 axes: nightlife 3→2, activity stays 3 but loses its top-line story. Final 30/39 puts it behind both Mallorca and Provence.

✓ What's still here — and it's still excellent

Michelin density unchanged: Arzak ★★★, Akelarre ★★★, Berasategui ★★★, Azurmendi ★★★ all open. Mugaritz ★★ open through Oct 25. Amelia ★★ in-house at Maria Cristina. Mirador de Ulía ★, Kokotxa ★, Alameda ★, Elkano ★ in Getaria — all operating. Pintxos crawl unchanged (Bar Néstor, Borda Berri, La Cuchara, Ganbara, La Viña — same kitchens, same hours, no queues). Activities unchanged: Pukas surf, La Perla thalasso, Three Beaches Geopark hike, Bilbao Guggenheim day, Rioja Alavesa, Hondarribia + France boat. Weather unchanged. If you want Donostia for its food and water — not its August festival energy — this week works.
