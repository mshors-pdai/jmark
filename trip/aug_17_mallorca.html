Festival de Pollença · Seong-Jin Cho solo piano

Wed Aug 19, 22:00 · Sant Domingo cloister · CONFIRMED

The week's marquee booking. Seong-Jin Cho — 2015 Chopin Competition winner, now one of the most-celebrated pianists of his generation — playing solo in Pollença's 17th-c Sant Domingo cloister on the 65th edition of one of Spain's most prestigious classical festivals. Outdoor cloister stage, the venue is the event. Confirmed on the 2026 programme. 55 min from Palma, 45 min from Deià. Tickets via festivalpollenca.koobin.com — book 30–60 days ahead; this date sells out first. This is the booking that justifies the whole week.

Sineu Wednesday Market — since 1318

Wed Aug 19, 8:00 → 13:30 · Mallorca's oldest agricultural fair

Mallorca's oldest agricultural fair, in continuous operation since 1318. Sineu (interior, 35 min from Palma) — Wednesday morning porc negre (black-pig) live auction in Sa Plaça, sobrasada straight from Felanitx black-pig producers, artisan cheese + EVOO + Manto Negro / Callet wine + leather + ceramics. Eat coca de patata + pa amb oli for breakfast at the bar tables in the square. Closes 13:30 sharp — get there by 8:30 for the auction + 11:00 for full breakfast crawl + leave by 13:00 for the wine route. The single most-photographed Wednesday in interior Mallorca.

Festival Chopin Valldemossa · final Sunday

Sun Aug 23, 21:30 · Real Cartuja cloister · LIKELY — verify Apr–May 2026

Sunday evening recitals in the Real Cartuja de Valldemossa — the 14th-c Carthusian monastery where Chopin + George Sand wintered 1838–39 and where Chopin composed the Op. 28 Preludes. The Sunday-night cloister format runs through August at 21:30; Aug 23 is the 4th Sunday and lines up with the pattern, but the 2026 programme is not yet confirmed. Verify festivalchopin.com Apr–May 2026 — note the SSL cert issue on the site mid-2026, fall back to email info@festivalchopin.com. Departure-day pivot: if confirmed, this is the trip's final-night cultural climax before the late MAD flight; if not, do Celler Sa Premsa in Palma.

Festa de Sant Bartomeu Sóller pre-festival

Sat Aug 22 + Sun Aug 23 · pre-festival activities (peak Aug 24 — miss by 1 day)

The Sant Bartomeu Sóller festival peaks Aug 24 — one day after departure (the day of the Firó mock-battle commemorating the 1561 Moorish raid). But the pre-festival activities run Aug 22–23: Sat Aug 22 Sóller morning mercadillo + evening concerts in Plaça Constitució; possibly Sun Aug 23 evening cossiers dance at Montuïri (the Montuïri cossiers are the only continuous traditional dance troupe on the island, traceable to 1821 — Aug 23 evening + Aug 24 morning is the traditional double). 25 min from Deià via the Sóller tunnel. Slot one Sóller evening into the Deià half of the week.

Cap de Formentor Road Closure

Closed to private cars 10:00 → 22:00 daily May 15 → Oct 15, 2026

The Ma-2210 lighthouse road is closed to private vehicles + motorcycles 10:00–22:00 daily May 15 → Oct 15, 2026 (corrected — not Oct 18). €100–200 fines for unauthorized access. Only TIB Bus 334, taxis, permits, cyclists, and hikers allowed in the closure window. Bus 334 last 20:30. If you want Cap de Formentor in daylight: drive before 10 AM (exit by 09:45) or sail in — the itinerary uses a full-day Mallorcan llaut from Port de Pollença on Day 4 because the from-the-water angle is the right one anyway and the cliff-jump bays are sea-access only.

Mon Aug 17

Arrival Palma · La Seu sunset · DINS Santi Taura ★ in-hotel · Abaco nightcap

  • 3:30 PM Land PMI · Iberia from Madrid · prearranged taxi (~€25, 15 min) to Hotel Es Princep on Parc de la Mar
  • 4:30 PM Check in · 5-star LHW · request a sea-view room facing the cathedral · the infinity rooftop pool + Almaq Bar have direct Catedral views
  • 5:30 PM Rooftop pool unwind · espresso at Almaq · feet up · jet-lag triage
  • 6:30 PM Walk 8 min to Catedral La Seu — Gothic giant with Gaudí's interventions + Miquel Barceló's 2007 ceramic apse chapel · note: cathedral interior closes 18:15 in Aug; if too late, walk the exterior + the Palau Reial de l'Almudaina grounds (Almudaina Aug hours 10:00–20:00, last entry 19:00)
  • 7:30 PM Stroll the Parc de la Mar reflecting pool — best cathedral photograph in town · continue into La Lonja old town
  • 8:15 PM Aperitif at Almaq (Es Princep rooftop) — first cathedral-view cocktail of the trip, ease into it
  • 9:00 PM Dinner at DINS Santi Taura ★ — in your hotel, El Llorenç Parc de la Mar setting. Single "Origens" tasting menu (11 courses + 3 optional), bar-counter format, watch the cooking. No children under 16. Reimagines traditional Mallorcan with deep technique. ~€110 + wine pairing. The natural arrival-night choice because you don't have to go anywhere.
  • 11:30 PM Walk 5 min through La Lonja's stone alleys to Bar Abaco, Calle San Juan 1 — the candle-lit Baroque cocktail bar in a 17th-century mansion. Birdcages, towers of fresh fruit + flowers, rococo mirrors, classical music, premium cocktails. "Most remarkable" cocktail bar per FT. Mandatory.
  • 1:00 AM Walk back to Es Princep — 8 min through Plaça de la Reina, harbor-lit
Alternative · Low energy swap: Skip Abaco. Have the DINS ★ tasting (already in your hotel), then nightcap at the Es Princep rooftop Almaq Bar with the cathedral floodlit below. In bed by midnight. Reasonable after the 11-hour LAX→MAD + connection.
Alternative · Doubling down swap: After Abaco, taxi (€10, 12 min) to Brooklyn in Santa Catalina — underground house/techno DJs spinning till 4 AM. The serious Palma club night. Brutal on jet-lag day but you'll never look back.
Alternative · Rainy day swap (low probability): Es Princep spa, then Es Baluard contemporary art museum (open till 21:00 in Aug, 5-min walk) for the Miró + Picasso + Mallorcan moderns collection. Then DINS ★ + Almaq.

Sineu Wed market 1318 · Bodega Ribas 1711 · Castell d'Alaró sunset · Pollença Cho recital 🎹

Palma → Deià · Cap Formentor sail · El Olivo ★ walk-in · Sa Fonda

  • 1:30 AM Walk 8 min back uphill to Es Molí — village is pin-drop quiet at 2 AM

Sat Aug 22

  • 11:30 AM Es Molí brunch on the panoramic terrace · pool

The serious GR221 hiking section (Deià → Sóller, 5 h moderate-tough via the Barranc de Biniaraix zigzag) is too brutal in Aug heat — save for spring.

The Glass

Two DOs · Manto Negro + Callet · plus Sineu market wine

Mallorca has two official Denominations of Origin: DO Binissalem (between Inca and Palma, the heritage zone) and DO Pla i Llevant (central + eastern, around Manacor and Felanitx, the experimentalists). Small but real — most don't export at scale, so the bottles you taste here are bottles you can only really drink here. The signature reds are made from Manto Negro (Binissalem) and Callet (Pla i Llevant cult variety, championed by Ànima Negra); the whites from Prensal Blanc and Moll. Plus one rogue: Vinyes Mortitx in the Tramuntana at 400 m — high-altitude mountain terraces. This week adds the Sineu Wednesday market — direct producer access for the same wines plus EVOO, sobrasada, cheese.

DO Binissalem · the heritage zone

Mallorca's First DO (1991) · Manto Negro

Older + larger of the two DOs, between Inca and Palma. Manto Negro dominates the reds (medium-bodied, savory, lower acidity); Prensal Blanc the whites (citrus, herbal, food-friendly). Closer to Palma (~25 min via MA-13) — easier wine-day target. The Day 3 wine route uses Ribas (1711, oldest) + Macià Batle (Santa Maria, polished tasting room) + the Sineu Wed market direct-producer access.
