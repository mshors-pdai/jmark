Only This Week

Don't-Miss Events on Your Exact Dates

Four experiences that only happen during the week you're there. The fireworks alone justify these dates.

🎆

Aste Nagusia · International Fireworks Competition

Aug 8–15 — your trip catches nights #5 → #8 (the finale)

The 60th International Fireworks Competition over La Concha Bay — nine nights, nine countries, one 22-minute pyrotechnic showcase each night at 10:45 PM. Free; you watch from Paseo de la Concha or your hotel-room balcony if you booked the bay-facing side. Sat Aug 15 is the finale + winners announcement — the loudest, longest, most-anticipated night. Stake a Paseo Nuevo spot from 9:30 PM with cava and pintxos to-go.

Aste Nagusia 2026 →
☄️

Perseid Meteor Shower Peak

Night of Wed Aug 12 → Thu Aug 13

The best Perseid year of the decade. New moon on Aug 12 = moonless sky, 90+ meteors/hour at peak (1–4 AM local). Drive 10 min up to Monte Igueldo — old amusement park closed by midnight but the parking lot has clear sky over the Atlantic. Bring a thermos of patxaran and a blanket. Or: skip the meteors for the Aste Nagusia fireworks earlier in the evening; you can't catch both at peak.

Viewing guide →
⛪

Asunción de la Virgen

Sat Aug 15 — Spanish national holiday

Spain's biggest summer holiday — banks, post offices, government closed; restaurants packed; the city completely full. Falls inside Aste Nagusia by design. Morning procession from Iglesia de Santa María del Coro through the old town. Book Saturday lunch and dinner 2+ months ahead. Aim for an early-evening pintxos crawl (6:30 PM) before the 10:45 PM fireworks finale.

SS Tourism agenda →
🚣

Bandera de la Concha · trainera heats

Mid-month rowing tests around La Concha

The traditional Basque rowing championship (final is early September) holds practice and heats in La Concha through August. Six-man wooden boats called traineras. Watch from Paseo de la Concha mid-morning. Free, unticketed. Pairs with a swim — water is 72°F, glassy, and ideal at 11 AM before the boats race.

🍇 Bonus — Rioja Alavesa day (the ambitious add-on)

90-min drive south of San Sebastián. Walk the medieval walled town of Laguardia, tour Bodegas Ysios (Calatrava's undulating roof), lunch at Marqués de Riscal (Gehry's titanium hotel). Hire a driver ($650–$975 for the day for two — required because you'll be tasting). Best on Day 5 (Sunday) when the city is recovering from the Aste Nagusia finale.

The Glass

Two Regions, 90 Minutes Apart

The salty Atlantic Txakoli of Getaria — poured high from the bottle to release the spritz — and the structured reds of Rioja Alavesa across the mountains. Both small, both serious.

Getariako Txakolina · the spritz whites

Spain's Smallest DO

Coastal Basque white made from Hondarribi Zuri, grown on terraces facing the Atlantic. Sharp, lemony, naturally spritzy. The pour is theater — the camarero holds the bottle two feet above the glass. 30 min west of San Sebastián by Euskotren. Best paired with grilled turbot at Elkano in Getaria.

Rioja Alavesa · the reds

Tempranillo Country — 90 min South

The Basque-administered side of Rioja, where the limestone-rich vineyards climb the southern slopes of the Cantabrian range. Medieval walled towns (Laguardia, Elciego) sit above caves stacked with barrels going back centuries. Hire a driver ($650–$975) — you'll be tasting, and the rural roads are not for the slightly-over-the-limit.

Marqués de Riscal ↗
Gehry's titanium-clad hotel + cellar built into the vineyards. Tour + lunch in the 1★ restaurant. Book online.
Bodegas Ysios ↗
Santiago Calatrava's undulating roof against the mountains. Daily tours 11 AM & 4 PM. €30.
Artadi ↗
Single-vineyard Tempranillos that command three-figure secondary-market prices. Visits by appointment only.
Remírez de Ganuza ↗
Boutique producer in Samaniego. Hand-selection at every stage. Tour + tasting by appointment.
