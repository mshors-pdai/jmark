Perseid Meteor Shower Peak — moonless

Night of Wed Aug 12 → Thu Aug 13 · new moon Aug 12

The decade's best Perseid year — moonless dark sky, 90+ meteors/hr at peak. Peak rates 02:00–04:00 local on Aug 13. ⚠️ Cap de Formentor is OFF-LIMITS — the Ma-2210 lighthouse road is closed to private vehicles 10:00–22:00 daily May 15–Oct 18, 2026 (€100–200 fines), and Bus 334 last departs 20:30. Festa de la Mare de Déu d'Agost

Fri Aug 14 + Sat Aug 15 — Mallorca's biggest mid-summer fiesta

Spain's biggest summer holiday + Mallorca's hardest public holiday. Banks + shops shut, coastal roads gridlock, restaurants packed. Aug 14 20:00 opening of "Els Llits de la Mare de Déu" (ephemeral altars across Palma + village churches). Aug 15 18:30 procession of cossiers with the dance "Gentil Señora". Three-day Palma old-town street party — orchestras in Plaça Major. Aug 15 = stay walking-distance in Palma OR stay-local in Deià. Do NOT plan Sa Calobra or any Tramuntana drive that day — coastal MA-10 will be bumper-to-bumper. The itinerary uses Aug 15 to swim Cala Deià + Sóller train round-trip + return to Palma 6 PM for the procession + late dinner.

Festival de Pollença 65th edition

Thu Aug 13 + Sat Aug 15 · Sant Domingo cloister 22:00

Mallorca's most prestigious classical music festival, 65th season — the open-air programme runs all August in the Sant Domingo cloister in Pollença. Thu Aug 13: Quartet Ébène (one of Europe's most-decorated string quartets). Sat Aug 15: Freiburger Barockorchester with violinist Éva Borchi and soprano Jeanine De Bique. 55 min from Palma, 45 min from Deià. Tickets via festivalpollenca.koobin.com — 30–60 days advance recommended for the marquee dates. Aug 15 conflicts directly with the Mare de Déu street party in Palma — pick one.

Festival Chopin Valldemossa

Sun Aug 16, 21:30 · Carthusian monastery cloister

Sunday evening recitals in the Real Cartuja de Valldemossa — the 14th-century Carthusian monastery where Chopin and George Sand wintered in 1838–39 — where Chopin composed the Preludes Op. 28. The Sunday-night cloister format runs through August at 21:30; verify the exact Aug 16 programme at festivalchopin.com (programme typically lands April–May). This is the trip's last-night cultural climax — 15 min from Belmond La Residencia, walk the monastery before or after.

Cap de Formentor Road Closure

Closed to private cars 10:00 → 22:00 daily May 15 → Oct 18, 2026

The Ma-2210 lighthouse road is closed to private vehicles 10:00–22:00 daily May 15–Oct 18, 2026. €100–200 fines for unauthorized access. Bus 334 from Port de Pollença runs through the day with last departure 20:30 — no public transit for Perseids overnight. If you want Cap de Formentor in daylight: drive in before 10:00 AM (early-morning lighthouse + Mirador es Colomer + walk to Cala Formentor) and exit by 09:45. If you want sunset views: take Bus 334. For Perseids: do NOT plan Formentor — use Mortitx / Lluc / Cap de ses Salines instead. The itinerary handles this by sailing the cliffs from Port de Pollença on Day 4 instead.

Deià International Music Festival

Runs May 2 → Oct 2, 2026 · across your full week

Smaller chamber-music festival staged across Deià's intimate venues — Son Marroig (Archduke Ludwig Salvator's 16th-c clifftop villa with the Carrara marble belvedere overlooking Sa Foradada), Miramar Monastery, and Deià Church. Programme runs May 2 – Oct 2, with multiple evening recitals per week through August. Slot into one of the two Deià evenings — Fri Aug 14 or Sat Aug 15 — if the programme aligns. Smaller crowd than Pollença or Chopin festivals; the venue itself (Son Marroig's marble rotunda over the Med) is the experience.

Where to Stay

Two Bases · 3 Palma + 2 Deià

Palma first (3 nights Tue–Fri for cathedral + nightlife + Michelin density + Mare de Déu lead-up), then Deià (2 nights Fri–Sun for cliff-village + spa + El Olivo ★ + Chopin recital). The 45-min Palma → Deià drive on Fri afternoon is the move. Two viable pairings, ~$6,000 apart.

Why Mallorca looks expensive — and how to bring it down

Mallorca's Standard $17,050 is almost entirely a Belmond La Residencia ceiling: $1,800–$3,300/night × 2 = $4,000–$6,600 just for the 2 Deià nights. Belmond La Residencia is not Mallorca — it's one hotel in one village. There are two honest pairings:

Marquee Pair · $17,050 Standard
Es Princep + Belmond La Residencia

The flagship pair. El Olivo ★ on-site at Belmond in the 17th-c olive oil mill; Joan Miró paintings on the walls; the Tramuntana hillside that's been the Mallorca-romance shorthand for 40 years. With Amex FHR 15% off + $100 F&B credit (book by May 15, 2026 for Jul–Aug) the 2 Belmond nights drop ~$1,200. Worth it if Belmond is the trip's reason.

Value Pair · ~$11,000 Standard
Es Princep + Hotel Es Molí

Same Palma base (cathedral views, DINS ★ + Zaranda ★ on-site). Same Deià village at a 17th-c hilltop mansion with panoramic Tramuntana + Med views, 4× daily shuttle to a private rocky cove, 32-yard pool, gardens. ~70% cheaper than Belmond per night. Saves ~$6,000 over 2 nights. Trade-off: 4★ instead of 5★, no El Olivo ★ on-site (eat there as a Belmond walk-in dinner instead, ~€200 less than staying). Same village. Same Cala Deià. Same Chopin recital.

The honest question: do you want Belmond, or do you want Mallorca? Belmond gives you both; Es Molí gives you Mallorca at 60% of the price, in the same village, with the savings freed up for Voro ★★ + an extra beach club day. If Belmond is the bucket-list item, take the Marquee pair; if Mallorca is the bucket-list item, take the Value pair. Both are listed first below.

The Glass

Two DOs · Manto Negro + Callet

Mallorca has two official Denominations of Origin: DO Binissalem (between Inca and Palma, the heritage zone) and DO Pla i Llevant (central + eastern, around Manacor and Felanitx, the experimentalists). Small but real — most don't export at scale, so the bottles you taste here are bottles you can only really drink here. The signature reds are made from Manto Negro (Binissalem) and Callet (Pla i Llevant cult variety, championed by Ànima Negra); the whites from Prensal Blanc and Moll. Plus one rogue: Vinyes Mortitx in the Tramuntana at 400 m — high-altitude mountain terraces that pair with the Perseids night drive.

DO Binissalem · the heritage zone

Mallorca's First DO (1991) · Manto Negro

Older + larger of the two DOs, between Inca and Palma. Manto Negro dominates the reds (medium-bodied, savory, lower acidity); Prensal Blanc the whites (citrus, herbal, food-friendly). Adversarial honesty — where Mallorca loses

Heat is real + dates are worst of year. Aug 8 historically wettest single day at 10.93 mm; sea always accessible but midday hiking in the Tramuntana (Castell d'Alaró, GR221) is brutal — dawn starts mandatory. Sa Calobra is bumper-to-bumper after 9 AM. Cap de Formentor lighthouse road is OFF-LIMITS to private cars 10:00–22:00 May 15–Oct 18, 2026 (€100–200 fines, Bus 334 last 20:30) — handle by sailing the cliffs from Port de Pollença instead.

Aug 15 Mare de Déu d'Agost is structural. Coastal MA-10 gridlocked, restaurants demand 14+ day reservations, beaches wall-to-wall. Forces the itinerary into a walking-Palma OR staying-Deià shape — the dual Cala Deià + Sóller + Palma-procession plan above is the densest packing of the day. The Aug 15 conflict between Festival Pollença (Freiburger Barockorchester) and the Palma procession is real — pick one. Most ★ tables book out 30+ days ahead for the Sat night specifically.

Two-hotel split has real cost. Belmond peak Aug $1,800–$3,300/night. 5 nights Belmond alone would top $15K; the Amex FHR 15% + $100 F&B drop the 2-night Belmond cost to ~$3,800. Es Princep + Belmond pair Standard ≈ $17,050 all-in for two. If hotels are the budget pinch, swap Belmond for Hotel Es Molí (same village, ~70% cheaper, 4★ instead of 5★) and the total drops to ~$10K — below Provence.

Deià is gorgeous but isolated for nightlife. Sa Fonda + Belmond bar only. Palma 45 min winding MA-10 road, taxis €80 each way. The 3+2 Palma+Deià split deliberately front-loads the city + nightlife so Deià doesn't feel nightlife-starved — by Day 4 you'll have already done Abaco + Sky Bar + Brooklyn (if you wanted), and Deià becomes the romance + scenery wind-down.

vs. San Sebastián: SS wins on food density (Arzak + Mugaritz + Akelarre + Etxebarri all ★★★-tier within 30 km + pintxos walkable) + cooler weather (~75°F). Mallorca wins on activity density + beaches + nightlife + romance scenery + the Joann-friendlier menu (SS is fish heaven; Mallorca is lamb-shoulder + sobrasada island).

vs. Mykonos: Mykonos beats outright on nightlife (Scorpios + Cavo Tagoo + Nammos = global top-10 beach clubs) and Cycladic whitewash romance. Loses on food (no ★★ on island), heat comparable, no Tramuntana hiking peer. Pure nightlife-maxxing = Mykonos; balanced active + food + nightlife = Mallorca.

vs. Côte d'Azur: CdA beats on glamour-nightlife (Nikki Beach St-Tropez + Caves du Roy) + Michelin star count in tight radius + Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc / La Réserve aspirational hotels. Loses on hiking/active density + price (CdA 30–40% more in Aug). Mallorca Tramuntana is the real differentiator.
