Belmond La Residencia · Deià

  • 16th-century manor in Deià village (Tramuntana, ~30 min from Palma).
  • Rates start $566–$822/night low season; peak-summer rooms have hit $3,294–$3,545 in recent Kayak bookings.
  • 15% Amex offer for July–Aug 2026 travel if booked by May 15, 2026.
  • Robert Graves' village; two pools, on-site spa, donkey statues, art collection.

Source: belmond.com · Amex FHR

August 15 Assumption Day

  • National public holiday — beaches and restaurants packed island-wide.
  • Religious processions and 'Els Llits de la Mare de Déu' (Virgin recumbent displays) in churches across Palma + many villages.
  • Cultural activities (music, dance, craft fairs) in Biniaraix, Caimari, Valldemossa, Sineu, Esporles, and others.
  • No central Palma fireworks tradition documented for the date — verify locally if fireworks are a must-have.

Source: In Mallorca Magazine · Sail Trip Mallorca

Climate · Aug

  • Real heatwave exposure during peak Aug — coastal/AC base mandatory.
