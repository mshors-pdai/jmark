Korčula Moreška sword dance · Mon Aug 17

Monday 17 Aug · 21:00 at Ljetno kino (Summer Theatre) — UNESCO-listed

The 500-year-old Korčula sword dance — UNESCO Intangible Heritage, dramatic battle between the White (Christian) and Black (Moor) kings over a captured princess, 7 escalating sword-clash sequences. Now performed at the Ljetno kino (Korčula Summer Theatre), not St Mark's Square as old guides claim. €20/pp adult. Logistics caveat: from Hvar this requires arriving Korčula by 18:00 and the last Korčula→Hvar ferry leaves at 17:40 — meaning you'd either need a private speedboat back (€800–€1,500 night charter) or sleep in Korčula and ferry back Tuesday morning. Only worth it if you slot it as the arrival-day bucket-list: fly into SPU, take an earlier Krilo Jet to Korčula direct (Split→Korčula ~3 h fast ferry, €30), watch the Moreška, sleep Korčula one night, ferry to Hvar Tuesday.

The Glass

Plavac Mali, Pošip, Vugava + Jelsa Wine Festival

Dalmatian wine is the trip's quiet upside surprise — small production, indigenous varieties, almost zero export. Plavac Mali (a Zinfandel cousin, the king of Dalmatian reds), Pošip (a citrus-mineral white from Korčula), and Vugava (Vis-only, aromatic) are the bottles you'll only really drink at the source. From Hvar Town you have two natural target zones: Hvar's own south-coast cliff vineyards (Sveta Nedjelja, Ivan Dolac) and a Vis day add-on (Lipanović's military-tunnel cellar). Plus this week's marquee: the Jelsa Wine Festival opens Thu Aug 20 and runs through Aug 30 — the first 4 nights overlap your stay, perfect harborfront sampling across producers in one walk.
